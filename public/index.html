Questions

Why can't I pick my own prize amount?

Nobody knows whether €2,720 or €6,470 is the right prize. Three sizes, each an order of magnitude apart, are easier to choose and easier for sellers to compare. You tune the difficulty with your scoring methods and rules instead.

Who checks the scores?

Participants report their own totals. Before any prize is paid, you check the winner against their profile on your platform. If you reject one, the prize moves to the next in line. If you don't answer within seven days, the winner counts as confirmed.

When does a contest start and end?

It starts the moment the payment is confirmed. Deadline contests run 7, 25 or 45 days. Score contests end when the first participant reaches 5, 25 or 100 points.

What is the lottery for?

Every day a participant updates their score earns them a ticket. That keeps everyone reporting, not just the leaders, so you can see how the contest is going.

How do I find participants?

You know best who should sell on your platform. Joridiro gives you the argument that convinces them to try it. Share the contest page with them directly, by mail, call or social media.
